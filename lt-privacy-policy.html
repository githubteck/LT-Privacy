LT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

LT is an Android TV media player for user-provided playlists and related programme-guide information. LT does not provide, host, sell, or bundle television channels, video streams, radio streams, playlists, or other media content.

1. Information LT uses

LT uses information that you provide or create while using the app, including playlist URLs or local playlist selections, EPG (electronic programme guide) URLs and data, favorites, recently watched/history information, playback positions, audio and subtitle selections, and app preferences.

If you choose to use the Discover feature, you may provide your own TMDB API key. LT uses that key to request movie and television metadata from TMDB.

2. Local storage

LT stores app settings and media-related state on your device so features such as favorites, playback resume, playlist configuration, programme-guide data, and preferences can work. LT also supports user-initiated backup and restore of supported LT settings and data.

3. Network connections and third-party services

When you configure a playlist, EPG source, artwork source, or media stream, LT connects to the network locations referenced by that content in order to retrieve or play the requested information. Those external services are operated by their respective providers and may receive standard network information such as your IP address as part of normal Internet communications.

The optional Discover feature uses TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. When Discover is used, requests are made to TMDB using the API key supplied by the user. TMDB's own privacy practices apply to its service.

4. LT Remote / local network features

LT may provide local-network functionality that lets another device on the same network send supported commands or setup information to LT. Information submitted through these features is used to perform the requested LT function. Users should use these features only on networks and devices they trust.

5. Advertising, sales, and accounts

LT Free 1.0 does not contain advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. LT does not require an LT user account.

6. Data sharing

LT does not sell personal information. Data is sent to third-party or user-specified services only as necessary to perform features requested by the user, such as downloading playlists or EPG information, playing media, retrieving artwork/metadata, or using TMDB Discover.

7. User control and deletion

You can remove configured playlists and supported app data through LT where those controls are provided. You can also remove locally stored LT data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling LT. Data held by third-party services is governed by those services' own policies.

8. Children

LT is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed or directed to children. Media available through LT depends on sources configured by the user.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LT's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.
